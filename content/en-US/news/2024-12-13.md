Development of the Everglow mod continued throughout July–December. Yggdrasil Town gained more areas to explore, Squamous Shell received more loot, and King Jelly Ball gained its own combat behaviors and music. Here are the additions and improvements from the past six months:

## New Content List

### World and Exploration

| Name | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Twilight Ruins | Added grass, soil layers, a castle, and battlements to the vast hollow surrounded by stone-scaled wood, further expanding the areas to explore in Yggdrasil Town |
| Living Lamp Wood | Added large branching trees with hollow trunks to the Lamp Wood forest, creating new landmarks |
| Qingduan Mining Area | Expanded the mines and tunnels beneath the town and incorporated them into town foundation generation |
| Yggdrasil Pylon Ruins | Added a ruins entrance near the main world's spawn point; right-clicking the pylon plays a teleportation sequence for travel to and from Yggdrasil |
| Stone Bridges and Cableways | Improved the stone bridges around Midnight Swamp and laid out a cableway to the right of the town toward the Source Pylon; cable car movement is still being adjusted, and boarding interactions need further work |
| Area Backgrounds | Added multilayer parallax backgrounds, transitions between areas, and day/night music selection to Midnight Swamp; added near and distant backgrounds to Yggdrasil Town |
| Lamp Wood Ruins Chests | Added exploration rewards to room chests; by the end of the year, the main reward pool had expanded to one of seven items: the amber weapons, Seven Shotgun, Yggdrasil Stone Gyroscope, and three exploration accessories |
| Rooms and Saving | Added room transitions and return logic to marble doors, with a generic wooden house temporarily used for interiors; Yggdrasil saving was enabled at the end of the year, but actual saving and return behavior still need verification |

### NPCs

| Name | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| ⭐Squamous Shell (BOSS) | Adjusted stats across difficulties and refined wingbeat, flight, and charge sounds; the seal on the map can summon the boss, and its rewards now include weapons, wings, Rock armor, an Expert Treasure Bag, and a relic |
| ⭐King Jelly Ball (BOSS) | Added combat behaviors including gel streams, absorbing jelly balls, and electrical discharges, along with dedicated music; the initial means of obtaining its summoning materials is still incomplete—stay tuned |
| Guard of Yggdrasil Town / Guard Slimy | Added a following companion and defensive spear and punching actions for the guard; how they first appear in normal play has yet to be confirmed |
| Lamp Fruit Borer | Can spawn naturally in Yggdrasil Town or fall in groups when Lamp Wood trees are felled; drops Lamp Borer Honey when defeated |
| Rock Elemental | Turns into a highly defensive rock on landing; when provoked, it draws in and throws terrain blocks or switches to charging attacks |
| Resturateur / Canteen Maid | Added character appearances and behaviors such as walking, sitting, and facing the player; these NPCs are still in development, and their shops, challenges, and quests are not yet available |

### Weapons

| Name | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Lamp Wood Bow / Lamp Wood Yoyo | Added basic bow and yoyo options; some materials and acquisition methods still need work |
| Bow of Enlightment / Handgun of Enlightment | Converts wooden arrows or ordinary bullets into light ammunition, applying a Photolysis effect on hit that depends on ambient brightness |
| Light Beam Staff | Beam hits can extend an existing Photolysis effect on the target, pairing with the light-based bow and handgun |
| Feathered Staff | Fires three feather projectiles at a time; they briefly home in before breaking apart and falling, and also produce fragment attacks on impact |
| Auburn Bell | Improved minion behavior: it idles around the player, then repeatedly aims, dashes, and slows down after spotting an enemy |
| Hyperock Spear | Charge level affects throw speed and damage; a highly charged hit attaches the spear to its target and draws in nearby enemies |
| Brittle Rock Slingshot | Hold to charge and release to fire; rocks break into smaller stones, with an additional explosion at high charge |
| Vitalized Rocks | Throws large and small rocks from beside the player, adding another rock-based magic attack |
| Amber-headed Spear / Amethyst-headed Spear | Added gemstone spears |
| Hyperion Amber Beam | Creates crystals at the end of the laser and briefly slows enemies on hit; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Hyperion Amber Orb | Fixed collision detection for the three-orb burst; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Nehemoth | Converts ordinary bullets into piercing rounds with a chance to inflict a disease that spreads to nearby enemies after dealing damage over time |
| Gun of Avarice | Holds 14 rounds; automatically reloading an empty magazine has a chance to increase its level and damage, while failure lowers its level and causes an explosion that damages the player; right-clicking to reload early is safe but resets its level |
| Eye of Anabiosis | Fires three magic projectiles that prioritize targets near the cursor; one of the rewards from Squamous Shell and its Treasure Bag |
| Dead Beetle Egg | Hatches a beetle that builds energy through melee hits, switches to magic attacks at full energy, and returns to melee when energy runs low; one of the rewards from Squamous Shell and its Treasure Bag |
| Seven Shotgun | Fires a spread of seven rounds of the current ammunition; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Yggdrasil Stone Gyroscope | Its minion steadily consumes energy, replenishing it by hitting enemies or being struck by the player's whip; higher energy increases damage; right-click to toggle the energy bar; found in Lamp Wood chests |
| Mysterious Tablet | Throwing it at an enemy increases that enemy's monetary value on hit; limits on repeated triggers and numerical balance still need work |

The following weapons also received new combat mechanics:

| Name | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| True Death Sickle | Reworked the six-hit slash combo; charge with right-click to unleash a close-range slash and a flying crescent blade |
| Terra Viewer Howitzer | Left-click for continuous fire, with an extra grenade falling from above every ten shots; right-click fires a large grenade with its own cooldown |
| Activated Jelly Gland | Deploys a short-lived minion that approaches enemies, charges up, then self-destructs and releases a beam aimed at its target |
| Kiss of Cthulhu | Spread shots have a chance to include a special round; accumulating necrotic buildup triggers burst damage and damage over time, with a bar showing the buildup |
| Nightfire Staff | Creates pairs of green firefly projectiles near the cursor that travel forward along opposing wavy paths |

| ---- | ---- |

| Molten Core | Grants bonuses such as damage and defense while affected by certain fire debuffs; damage reduction values are still behaving incorrectly and need adjustment |

| Forsaken Boots | Improves movement speed and running ability |
| Anemo Shell | Automatically deploys protection near the ground during a dangerous fall and bounces the player upward, then enters a one-minute cooldown shown on the item in the inventory |
| Celtic Seal | Restores 15 life after taking two hits of at least 6 damage each from enemies or projectiles; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Conceal Spell | Charges faster while stationary, gradually turns the player invisible as it nears full charge, and negates one hit at full charge; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Lamp Wood Pollen Bottle | Grants two extra jumps; one of the main rewards from Lamp Wood chests |
| Jelly Ball Inhibitor | Increases damage against three specified types of gel enemies and reduces contact damage taken from them |
| Fragments of Wreckage | Increases damage, melee speed, defense, and mining ability as life is lost |
| Shackles Ball | Trades movement and jumping ability for damage reduction and grants immunity to Slow |
| Recovery Band | Restores a small amount of life and mana after a single hit deals at least 5 damage |
| Funerary Goods | Added a chance to restore mana on a kill; whether the effect actually triggers still needs verification |
| Skull Collection | Grants a brief mana regeneration buff when enemies are nearby |

### Armor

| Name | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Rock | Wearing the full set builds up to 12 extra defense through movement, which is cleared on taking damage; set pieces were added to the rewards from Squamous Shell and its Treasure Bag |
| Lamp Wood | Shifted its focus from defense to mining, life regeneration, and lighting, while retaining its Lamp Wood crafting recipes |
| Twilight Wood | Added ranged damage, critical strike, and ammunition conservation effects; acquisition methods and resetting its effects after unequipping still need work |
| Auburn | Added the full set's extra minion slots; its active enhancement is not yet connected |
| Jelly Ball Bathing Cap | Headgear that adds minion slots and has a small chance to reduce damage from and reflect incoming projectiles |

## Cooking

| Feature | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Kitchen Management | Implemented a separate kitchen room, ingredient collection, timed orders, serving dishes at the counter, stage completion, and bonus payouts; the kitchen entrance in normal worlds has yet to be confirmed |
| Clay Pot Dishes | Improved clay pot cooking, with a menu including mapo tofu, fish-fragrant eggplant, and boiled bullfrog; some dishes temporarily share the same buffs |
| Steamers and Xiaolongbao | Stoves can be fitted with single- or double-tier steamers; added the process of turning raw xiaolongbao into steamed buns |
| Orders and Recipes | Added ingredient hints, order cancellation, and a compact view; organized recipe matching rules and added support for groups of substitute ingredients |
