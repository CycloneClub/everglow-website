Development of the Everglow mod continued throughout July–December. Yggdrasil Town gained more areas to explore, Squamous Shell received more loot, and King Jelly Ball gained its own combat behaviors and music. Here are the additions and improvements from the past six months:

## New Content List

### World and Exploration

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| ---- | --- | ---- |
| Twilight Ruins | 暮光遗迹 | Added grass, soil layers, a castle, and battlements to the vast hollow surrounded by stone-scaled wood, further expanding the areas to explore in Yggdrasil Town |
| Living Lamp Wood | 生命灯木 | Added large branching trees with hollow trunks to the Lamp Wood forest, creating new landmarks |
| Qingduan Mining Area | 青缎矿区 | Expanded the mines and tunnels beneath the town and incorporated them into town foundation generation |
| Yggdrasil Pylon Ruins | 天穹树晶塔遗迹 | Added a ruins entrance near the main world's spawn point; right-clicking the pylon plays a teleportation sequence for travel to and from Yggdrasil |
| Stone Bridges and Cableways | 石桥与索道 | Improved the stone bridges around Midnight Swamp and laid out a cableway to the right of the town toward the Source Pylon; cable car movement is still being adjusted, and boarding interactions need further work |
| Area Backgrounds | 区域背景 | Added multilayer parallax backgrounds, transitions between areas, and day/night music selection to Midnight Swamp; added near and distant backgrounds to Yggdrasil Town |
| Lamp Wood Ruins Chests | 灯木遗迹宝箱 | Added exploration rewards to room chests; by the end of the year, the main reward pool had expanded to one of seven items: the amber weapons, Seven Shotgun, Yggdrasil Stone Gyroscope, and three exploration accessories |
| Rooms and Saving | 房间与存档 | Added room transitions and return logic to marble doors, with a generic wooden house temporarily used for interiors; Yggdrasil saving was enabled at the end of the year, but actual saving and return behavior still need verification |

### NPCs

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| ---- | --- | ---- |

| ⭐King Jelly Ball (BOSS) | ⭐球冻王(BOSS) | Added combat behaviors including gel streams, absorbing jelly balls, and electrical discharges, along with dedicated music; the initial means of obtaining its summoning materials is still incomplete—stay tuned |

| Rock Elemental | 磐石元素 | Turns into a highly defensive rock on landing; when provoked, it draws in and throws terrain blocks or switches to charging attacks |
| Resturateur / Canteen Maid | 餐馆老板与食堂女仆 | Added character appearances and behaviors such as walking, sitting, and facing the player; these NPCs are still in development, and their shops, challenges, and quests are not yet available |

## Cooking

| Feature | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Kitchen Management | Implemented a separate kitchen room, ingredient collection, timed orders, serving dishes at the counter, stage completion, and bonus payouts; the kitchen entrance in normal worlds has yet to be confirmed |
| Clay Pot Dishes | Improved clay pot cooking, with a menu including mapo tofu, fish-fragrant eggplant, and boiled bullfrog; some dishes temporarily share the same buffs |
| Steamers and Xiaolongbao | Stoves can be fitted with single- or double-tier steamers; added the process of turning raw xiaolongbao into steamed buns |
| Orders and Recipes | Added ingredient hints, order cancellation, and a compact view; organized recipe matching rules and added support for groups of substitute ingredients |

## Updates to Existing Content

| Feature | Description |
| ---- | ---- |
| Lantern Moon | Replaced automatic score increases with combat-based progression and added Lantern Ghost King at wave 15; added Explosive Lanterns and Cylindrical Lanterns, the latter of which teleport and release columns of fire in two directions upon death; the recipe for starting the event is still incomplete, and later waves need further work |
| Lantern Ghost King | Reworked the fight with a ring-of-fire boundary that damages the player on contact, plus barrages of streaming lanterns from the perimeter and above |

| Club Weapons | Established left-click for swinging and right-click for downward slams, and added distinct heavy-hit effects to Orichalcum, Adamantite, Titanium, and Mythril clubs; a later fix addressed compatibility between downward slams and water walking |
| Acytaea | Adjusted idle, dialogue, and sitting behaviors, fixing sitting poses and seat occupancy after a chair is removed; improved attack target selection while retaining the snow biome exception |
| Corrupt Moth | Fixed the loading path for its combat music |
