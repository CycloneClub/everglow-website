## New Content List

### Areas and Scenery

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Bayou | 黑沉沼泽 | A new swamp area with bridges, warning signs, and vast stretches of sludge |
| Twilight Castle | — | Random combinations of themed rooms, with new dark dragon-themed rooms and mechanisms that launch homing attacks |
| Death Jade Lake | 亡碧湖 | Improved underwater backgrounds and the atmosphere at the water’s surface, with backgrounds fading in and out as you move between areas |
| Underwater Treasury / Underwater Dungeon | 水下机关关卡 | Flooded rooms, passages, platforms, and traps form an exploration route; in development |

### Weapons

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Razorbeak Bow | 尖喙弓 | Gains an attack speed boost when all three arrows in a volley hit, stacking up to eight times; resets after too long a break |
| DevilHeart Gyroscope | 魔心陀螺 | A summoning weapon that can be charged with a whip and has its own energy bar |
| Evil Halbert Barnacle | 魔戟藤壶 | A melee weapon; left-click to attack, or hold right-click to aim and release to fire |
| Activated Dog Staff | 活化之犬召唤杖 | A summoning weapon that summons a dog after a brief hatching period; the dog automatically seeks out and dashes at enemies |
| Arc I | 电弧一号 | A ranged weapon that builds one electric charge every five shots, storing up to three charges; charges can be consumed to add electric strikes |
| Empty Water Staff | 空水杖 | A magic weapon that traps ordinary enemies susceptible to knockback in bubbles; bubbles created underwater last twice as long |
| Chain Grenade | — | A bouncing grenade that splits into three smaller grenades after the main explosion, creating a chain of blasts |
| Introduction to Thermal Electricity | 热电法术绪论 | A magic weapon whose electric orbs create an expanding electric field when they hit an enemy or wall, or enter water |
| Pear Shaped Needle | — | A ranged weapon that prepares an array of needles while held and fires the prepared needles on release |
| Spring of Quicksand | 流沙泉 | A magic weapon whose projectile trails a downward-extending curtain of quicksand |

### Miscellaneous

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geyser Air Bud | 间歇泉气苞 | Launches the player upward when stepped on and can trigger again after a cooldown |
| Holographic Pylon / Town Gate Pylon | — | Pylons with relaxed restrictions on NPC count, danger, and biome |
| Unregistered Pylon | — | Right-click to unlock it as a Holographic Pylon, with an unlocking effect |
| Escalator | 扶梯 | Touch a step and press Up to ride with it; press Down to get off |

### Underwater Mechanisms (In Development)

| Name | Chinese Name | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IR Probe | 探针 | Detects players and triggers connected mechanisms |
| Underwater Guillotine | 水下断头台 | Drops when it receives a wire signal; pass with care! |
| Lightning Mechanism | 电击机关 | Creates a dangerous electric arc between the mechanisms at either end when triggered |
| Sponge Oxygen Bubble | 供氧气泡 | Produced by a Drain Outlet to replenish the player’s breath |
