Development of the Everglow mod continued throughout January–June. From the massive furnace in the Furnace Area to Fevens’s multiphase battle and new summoning weapons and mining tools, exploration and interaction in Yggdrasil Town continued to grow, while the Kelp Curtain gained more equipment options. Here is a look at the new content, gameplay updates, and improvements to existing content from this period.

This edition’s cover shows the Furnace Area: a towering furnace, intersecting pipes, and orange flames form an industrial landscape within the town.

## Gameplay Updates

### Yggdrasil Arena

Yggdrasil Town now has a dedicated arena. Players can take on challenges in a special room and use modifiers to change the combat conditions: reduce their damage, defense, or maximum life; restrict healing potions; or prohibit pickaxe use and the placement of blocks and walls.

The arena also introduces active blocking. Blocking an attack at the right moment displays “Block!” and grants brief protection. Alongside equipment choices, the timing of your defenses is now part of the challenge.

### Furnace Resource Contributions

The town’s massive furnace now accepts resources. Pressing the smelt button consumes items from nearby chests and converts them into personal furnace points and system energy based on their value, rarity, and quantity.

Personal points can be saved, while energy is consumed as the furnace runs. Before contributing resources, remember to remove any items you want to keep from nearby chests!

### Cooking Ranges and Steamer Baskets

The Chinese Cooking Range can be fitted with steamer baskets. Hold a steamer basket and right-click the range to install cookware step by step, then open the corresponding cooking interface. The range also displays prompts based on the cookware you are holding, adding more interaction to kitchen furniture.

### New Mining and Summoning Controls

- **Charged mining**: Hold right-click with the Mining Power Pickaxe to charge it, then left-click to mine. With sufficient pickaxe power and charge, it can mine multiple connected ore blocks at once.
- **Commanded detonation**: Evil Music Remnant summons can fight continuously or be ordered to pursue and detonate, making them useful when you need concentrated damage.
- **Mycelium and fields**: The Woodland Wraith Staff can place a spore field to empower minions. After gaining regeneration by standing on mycelium, entering your own field further strengthens the effect.
