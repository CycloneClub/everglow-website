## Get Started

Read the [installation guide](https://everglowmod.com/en-us/docs/getting-started/installation){target="_self"}, get Everglow from the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2906178094) or [development releases](https://github.com/CycloneClub/Everglow/releases/tag/dev), and follow the guide to complete the installation.

## Contributing

Code, documentation, translations, and design improvements are welcome. Read the [contribution guide](https://github.com/CycloneClub/Everglow/blob/1.4.4/CONTRIBUTING.md) before getting started, and follow the [Code of Conduct](https://github.com/CycloneClub/Everglow/blob/1.4.4/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) in all community spaces.

[Report a problem or suggest an improvement](https://github.com/CycloneClub/Everglow/issues/new), or contribute changes through a pull request.

Join our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/pdXvp89Dbp) to talk with the team and other tModLoader players.
