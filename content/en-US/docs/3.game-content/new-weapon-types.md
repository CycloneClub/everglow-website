Everglow brings new ways to fight with a range of weapons. The sections below introduce these weapon types and changes to melee animations.

## Clubs

Hold left-click to keep a club spinning. Its rotation gradually speeds up, increasing its damage, while hitting enemies costs some of that speed.

When you are high enough above a landing surface, release left-click and right-click to plunge downward, then follow through with a swing on landing. A longer plunge produces a more powerful landing attack.

## Rapiers

Hold left-click to thrust repeatedly toward the cursor, or right-click for a stronger thrust. Both attacks consume stamina, which gradually recovers when you stop attacking.

If your stamina runs out, you must wait for it to recover fully before attacking again. Pausing between flurries and choosing when to use a heavy thrust helps you manage it.

## Slingshots

Hold left-click to draw and charge the slingshot, then release to launch a projectile toward the cursor. A fuller charge increases both projectile speed and damage.

The Wood Slingshot, for example, supplies its own projectiles and requires no ammunition, leaving you free to focus on aiming and timing your release.

## Spinning Tops

Spinning tops are summon weapons that chase enemies close to the ground. When there is no target, they stay near you.

The Yggdrasil Stone Gyroscope gradually loses spinning power. Striking it with a whip or letting it hit enemies replenishes that power. More power makes it faster and stronger; when its power runs out, it disappears. Right-click while holding the Yggdrasil Stone Gyroscope to toggle its power bar.

## Whips (Reworked)

Whips swing outward, extend, and retract, letting summoners take an active role in combat. Each whip can bring its own effects on hit.

The Crimson Moon Algae Whip applies red algae toxin, which detonates when the target is hit again. The longer the toxin remains before that hit, the stronger the burst. If it expires naturally, it deals no damage.

## Improved Melee Animations

Some melee weapons have dedicated attack animations, with weapon paths, character poses, and impact effects that change throughout the attack.

With the Quenching Blade, for example, holding left-click chains slashes into a rising attack and a heavy slam. Pay attention to the windup and swing direction to keep enemies within reach.

True Death Sickle chains slashes together. You can also hold right-click to prepare an attack, then release to slash and launch a crescent blade.
