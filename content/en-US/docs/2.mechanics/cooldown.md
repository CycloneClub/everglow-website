Everglow displays cooldown indicators for certain equipment effects and abilities, helping you see when they can trigger again.

## Overview

Some effects require a cooldown after activation before they can trigger again. When the cooldown ends, its indicator disappears.

A cooldown is not necessarily the same length as the effect itself: an effect ending does not mean it is ready to trigger again.

### Cooldown UI

Cooldown icons appear below the buff icons in the upper-left corner of the screen. Multiple cooldowns are arranged horizontally. The circular indicator and pointer show the remaining progress, while the text below shows the time remaining.

Hover over an icon to view its name and description. The icon gradually brightens while hovered. During boss fights, the icons stay bright so they are easier to see in combat.

Opening your inventory temporarily hides these indicators. Close it to see any cooldowns that are still active.
