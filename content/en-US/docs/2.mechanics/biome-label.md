Everglow displays a label when you enter a new biome, helping you identify the area you are exploring.

## Overview

As you explore and enter a new biome, its name and icon automatically appear at the top of the screen. This label tells you which biome you have just entered.

### Biome Label UI

The biome icon appears on the left, with its name on the right. For example, entering a snowy area displays the Snow biome's name and icon.

## Where Labels Appear

### Modded Biomes

Biome labels support biomes added by Everglow and other mods. Each biome can display its own icon; biomes without a dedicated icon use a default one.

### Vanilla Biomes

Vanilla biomes also have name labels, with icons in a consistent style. These labels only appear in the main world; vanilla biome labels are not shown in subworlds.
